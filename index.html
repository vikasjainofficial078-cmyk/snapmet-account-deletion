SnapMet Account Deletion

This page explains how SnapMet users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to vikasjainofficial078@gmail.com from the email address associated with your SnapMet account, or include your registered phone number/user ID if you registered another way.
  2. Use the subject line SnapMet Account Deletion Request.
  3. We may ask you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Important: Replace the placeholder support email on this page with an email address that you control before publishing. The email address must work so users can actually submit deletion requests.

What data is deleted?

After we verify and process your request, we will delete or de-identify your account profile and personal data associated with the account, except data that we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or accounting purposes.

How long does deletion take?

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days. If some information must be retained for a valid legal or security reason, it will be retained only as permitted or required and handled according to our applicable retention practices.
